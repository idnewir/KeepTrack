# Reports Guide

Reports let you produce a clear, professional summary of the finances — perfect for sharing with a committee, trustees, or just for your own records. Everyone can run and export reports, no matter their role.

## Step 1: Choose what to include

1. Go to the **Reports** page from the sidebar.
2. Pick a **date range** — for example, the last three months, or the whole financial year so far.
3. Optionally, pick one or more **categories** to focus on (for example, just "Electricity" and "Water"), or leave this blank to include everything.

## Step 2: Choose the type of report

- **Historical analysis** — a summary of what has already happened in the period you chose.
- **Forecast** — a look ahead at what's expected to happen, including any planned projects.

## Step 3: Let Keep Track write the summary

Keep Track uses AI to read through all the relevant figures and write a plain-English summary explaining the key points — for example, whether spending is on track, which categories cost the most, or whether a planned project will affect the reserve.

## Step 4: Review and export

1. Have a read through the report on screen.
2. When you're happy, click **Export PDF**.
3. A polished PDF document will be downloaded to your device, ready to print, email, or present.

## Tips

- Reports always use the same design as Keep Track's standard report template, so they'll look consistent every time.
- You can run as many reports as you like — creating a report doesn't change any of your data.
- If a report doesn't look right, double-check that the invoices and contributions for that period have been entered correctly first.
