# Dashboard Guide

The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in. It gives you a quick picture of the current financial position.

## The main chart

In the middle of the page you'll see a chart with three lines:

- **Income** — money that's come in from contributions.
- **Expenses** — money that's actually been spent (confirmed invoices).
- **Forecast** — an estimate of what's likely to be spent for the rest of the year, based on past patterns and any planned projects.

The chart covers the whole financial year, from September to August.

**Tip:** You can click on any part of the chart to see more detail.
- Click the **income** line to see a table of contributions, split by group and month.
- Click the **expenses** line to see a full list of invoices, which you can filter by category or date.
- Click the **forecast** line to see a breakdown of what's expected, by category.

Planned projects (big one-off costs that are being planned) show up on the forecast line with a different colour and a marker, so you can tell them apart from routine running costs.

## The reserve gauge

This is a simple dial that shows whether the current balance is healthy:

- **Green** — balance is comfortably above target.
- **Amber** — balance is getting close to the target and worth keeping an eye on.
- **Red** — balance has dropped below the target reserve.

The target is worked out automatically from the average spend over the last three months.

## The panels

Below the main chart you'll find several smaller panels:

- **Upcoming expected invoices** — a heads-up on invoices Keep Track expects soon, based on when they've arrived in the past.
- **Recent activity** — the last few things that happened (invoices added, contributions recorded, and so on).
- **Planned projects** — a list of upcoming projects with their estimated cost and when they're expected.

## Quick action buttons

For the things you do most often, there are shortcut buttons right on the dashboard:

- **Upload invoice**
- **Record contribution**
- **Run report**

## Notifications

When you log in, you may see a notification banner if something needs your attention, such as:

- The balance has dropped below target.
- A monthly reconciliation is overdue.
- An invoice has been waiting for review for a while.
- A possible duplicate invoice has been flagged.
- An invoice you'd normally expect by now hasn't arrived.

Click the notification to go straight to the relevant page.
