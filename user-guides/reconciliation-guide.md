# Reconciliation Guide

Reconciliation is the monthly check to make sure Keep Track's numbers match what the bank actually shows. This guide walks you through it.

## Who can do this

Administrators and Standard users can carry out reconciliation. Read-only users can view past reconciliations but cannot add new ones.

## Why it matters

Keep Track works out a "calculated balance" automatically, based on the opening balance plus contributions minus invoices. But mistakes happen — an invoice might be missed, or a contribution forgotten. Reconciliation catches these before they build up.

## Step-by-step

1. Go to the **Reconciliation** page from the sidebar.
2. Keep Track shows you the **calculated balance** for the month — this is what the app thinks the balance should be.
3. Get your bank statement (or online banking app) and find the actual balance at the end of the month.
4. Type that figure into the **actual balance** box.
5. Click **Compare**.

## If the numbers match

Great — nothing more to do. Click **Save** to record the reconciliation for the month.

## If the numbers don't match

Keep Track will show you the difference (the "discrepancy") and suggest a likely reason, such as:

- An invoice that hasn't been added yet.
- A contribution that wasn't recorded.
- A bank charge or interest payment that isn't in Keep Track.

1. Read the suggestion and check it against your records.
2. If you find and fix the cause (for example, by adding a missing invoice), come back and reconcile again.
3. If you can't find the cause straight away, you can still save the reconciliation with a note explaining what you know so far — you can update it later.

## After reconciling

The confirmed closing balance for the month automatically becomes next month's opening balance, so everything stays lined up going forward.

## Tips

- Try to reconcile every month, as soon as your bank statement is available — small discrepancies are much easier to track down quickly than after several months have passed.
- If reconciliation is overdue, you'll see a reminder notification when you log in.
